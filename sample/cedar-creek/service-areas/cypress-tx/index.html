What we see in Cypress homes

Water heaters hitting their age

Lots of Cypress homes were built in the 2000s and 2010s. Water heaters usually last 8–12 years, so many original units are due for replacement.

Hard water

Many Cy-Fair homes have hard water, which leaves sediment in water heaters and scale on fixtures. A yearly flush helps.

Slab foundations

Most homes here sit on concrete slabs, so we’re set up for slab leak detection without tearing up floors.
