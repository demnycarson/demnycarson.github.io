Is it one drain or the main line?

If one sink is slow, the clog is usually close to that drain. If several drains are acting up at once, the problem is likely in your main sewer line, and that needs attention before it backs up into the house.

Warning signs of a main line clog:

  • Water comes up in the tub when you flush a toilet
  • Several drains are slow at the same time
  • Gurgling sounds from drains or toilets
  • Sewer smell inside or near the outdoor cleanout

Drain Cleaning questions

Are chemical drain cleaners safe for my pipes?

We don’t recommend them. They often don’t clear the clog fully, and repeated use can damage older pipes. A snake or hydro jetting removes the clog instead of eating at it.

What causes most kitchen sink clogs?

Grease poured down the drain. It cools and hardens in the pipe, then catches food scraps. Wipe greasy pans with a paper towel before washing.

What is a sewer camera inspection?

We feed a small waterproof camera through your drain line to find roots, breaks, sags or buildup. You see the video too, so you know exactly what’s going on.

How can I prevent clogs?

Keep grease and wipes out of drains, use hair catchers in showers, and run hot water after using the garbage disposal.
