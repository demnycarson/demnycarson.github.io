One simple price: $150 per month. That covers a custom new website, mobile-friendly design, local SEO and AI search setup, ongoing updates and fixes, and a monthly report.

I build brand-new websites. If your current site is outdated, I'll replace it with a new one built from scratch.

Will my business show up on Google?

Every site is built for search: clear page titles, service and location details, and the code Google reads. Search results usually take a few months to build, and no one can honestly guarantee a #1 spot.

Can you help my business show up in ChatGPT and AI search?

Yes. Every plan includes AI search setup, so tools like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and Gemini can understand what your business does and where you work.
