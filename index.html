07 — FAQ

Common questions.

How much does a website cost?

One simple price: $397 per month. That covers a custom multi-page website, hosting, your domain, SEO and AI search setup, and monthly work: a new page or FAQ update, 2 Google Business Profile posts, review reply help, small edits and a report.

Is there a contract?

The first 3 months are a minimum, since that covers building your site. After that it's month-to-month and you can cancel anytime with 30 days' notice.

Who owns my domain?

I buy and manage your domain as part of the plan, registered for your business. If you ever cancel, I transfer it to you, so your business name is always yours.

What happens to my website if I cancel?

The site is part of the monthly plan, so it comes down when the plan ends. If you'd like to keep it, you can buy the site files for a one-time $500 and host them anywhere.

Do you build new websites or fix old ones?

I build brand-new websites. If your current site is outdated, I'll replace it with a new one built from scratch.

Will my business show up on Google?

Every site is built for search: clear page titles, service and location details, and the code Google reads. Search results usually take a few months to build, and no one can honestly guarantee a #1 spot.

Can you help my business show up in ChatGPT and AI search?

Yes. Every plan includes AI search setup, so tools like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and Gemini can understand what your business does and where you work.

Do you work with businesses outside Texas?

Yes. I work with businesses anywhere in the U.S. Everything happens by phone, email, text and video call, so you never need to meet in person. I set up search for the areas you actually serve, whether that's one city or nationwide.
