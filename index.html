FAQ

Common questions.

How much does a website cost?

$397 a month, with no setup fee. That covers everything: your custom website, hosting, your domain name, getting found on Google and AI search, your Google Business Profile, getting more reviews, and monthly updates like posts, photos, review replies, edits and progress reports. Want to save? Pay upfront: $1,131 for 3 months, $2,202 for 6 months, or $4,284 for 12 months.

Is there a contract?

No long contract. The monthly plan is month-to-month and you can cancel anytime. If you want a discount, you can prepay for 3, 6 or 12 months instead.

Who owns my domain?

I buy and manage your domain as part of the plan, registered for your business. If you ever cancel, I transfer it to you, so your business name is always yours.

What happens to my website if I cancel?

The site is part of the monthly plan, so it comes down when the plan ends.

Do you build new websites or fix old ones?

I build brand-new websites. If your current site is outdated, I'll replace it with a new one built from scratch.

Will my business show up on Google?

Yes, every site is built to show up on Google. Each page clearly says what you do and where you do it, in a way Google understands. It usually takes a few months to climb, and no one can honestly promise a #1 spot.

Can you help me rank on Google Maps?

Yes. I set up and fix up your Google Business Profile (your categories, services, hours, photos and service areas), then keep it active every month with posts, updates and new reviews. I also connect it to your website so Google trusts both. Climbing the map takes time and depends on your area and competition. No one can honestly promise a top-3 spot, but an active, complete profile gives you the best shot.

Can you help my business show up in ChatGPT and AI search?

Yes. Every plan includes AI search setup. So when someone asks ChatGPT, Gemini or Google’s AI for a business like yours, those tools know what you do and where you work.

Do you work with businesses outside Texas?

Yes. I work with businesses anywhere in the U.S. Everything happens by phone, email, text and video call, so you never need to meet in person. I set you up to show up in the areas you actually serve, whether that's one city or the whole country.
