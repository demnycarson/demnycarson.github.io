Why Your Business Isn't Showing Up on Google (and How to Fix It)

By Carson Demny · October 28, 2026 · 5 min read

You search for your own business on Google and… nothing. Or you show up, but way down the list behind your competitors. Here are the most common reasons this happens, and what to do about each one.

1. You don't have a Google Business Profile (or it's not verified)

Without a verified profile, you basically can't show up on Google Maps. Fix: go to business.google.com, claim or create your listing, and finish verification. Until it's verified, it won't show up properly.

2. Your profile is incomplete

Missing categories, services, hours or photos make it harder for Google to match you to searches. Fix: fill out every section, pick the most specific main category, and add real photos.

3. Your website is missing, outdated or hard to use on a phone

Google looks at your website to understand what you do. If you don't have one, it's slow, or it's one page that barely mentions your services, you're giving Google very little to work with. Fix: a fast, phone-friendly site with a page for each service and the areas you serve.

4. Your info doesn't match everywhere

If your name, phone number or address are different on your website, Google profile, Facebook and directories like Yelp, it creates confusion. Fix: pick one exact version and update it everywhere.

5. You have few (or old) reviews

Businesses with more recent reviews tend to look more trusted, both to Google and to customers. Fix: ask every happy customer for a review, and reach out to past customers too. Here's how to do it the right way.

6. You're not using the words customers search for

If people search “water heater repair” but your site only says “plumbing solutions,” you're missing matches. Fix: use plain, specific words for each service on your website and in your Google profile.

7. Your profile is inactive

A profile that hasn't been touched in a year looks abandoned. Fix: post updates and add photos every month.

8. You're competing in a crowded area

In big cities, dozens of businesses fight for the same few spots. Fix: focus on the specific services and neighborhoods you're best at, and be consistent every month. Read how to rank higher on Google Maps for the full plan.

Quick test: search for your main service plus your city on your phone. Are you on the map? In the top 3? That's where most calls go.
